Project Description: Luminous Harmony at Trade House, Payyannur, Kannur. This project seamlessly combines modern architecture with a warm, serene ambiance.
Key Features: Magnetic Linear Track Lighting provides a modern, minimal look in the living area and hallway. Focused Accent Spotlight Lighting highlights specific art and architectural features. Recessed Downlights enhance the natural warmth of wooden ceilings. Cove and Strip Lighting create soft, indirect lighting effects. A Modern Sphere Pendant Chandelier serves as a striking centerpiece in the living room. Wall Washer Effects provide depth to textured walls.
Specifications: Project Name: Rajesh Lights. Location: Payyannur, Kannur. Lighting Elements: Magnetic tracks, COB spotlights, slim profile lights, and a spherical cluster pendant. Color Temperature: 3000K Warm White. Interior Theme: Contemporary Wooden & Earthy.
